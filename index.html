A personal household integration

Your thermostat.
Your Home Assistant.

Home Assistant Nest connects the account owner's selected Google Nest thermostat to their own Home Assistant installation, using Google's authorized device APIs.

Read the privacy policy ↗

01

See household conditions

View the selected thermostat's temperature, humidity, operating state and supported settings in Home Assistant.

02

Use supported controls

Allow the owner to adjust supported thermostat settings through the authorized Home Assistant installation.

03

Receive device updates

Use Google Pub/Sub topics and subscriptions to deliver device updates to Home Assistant.

Access with the owner's permission

A clear purpose for each permission.

Google authorization covers the thermostat the owner selects, its readings and settings, permitted home information such as home and room names and ZIP code, and Google Pub/Sub access for device updates.

Google processes API and event traffic. Home Assistant processes the readings and controls, and may retain information in configured history, logs and backups. Owner-configured connected services, including assistants, may also receive or access information under the owner's configuration.

The privacy policy explains processing, storage, connected services and how to revoke access.

For one owner's household
